Rollback

Stop and disable buck's service with systemctl disable --now wg-quick@recoilnet.
Remove its UFW rule with:
sudo ufw delete allow in on recoilnet from fd96:57e3:eb75::/48 to fd96:57e3:eb75:1::101 port 22 proto tcp
Deactivate and delete lack's recoilnet tunnel in the WireGuard app.
On dark, remove the overlay route, destroy wg0 and remove /etc/hostname.wg0.
Remove the Recoilnet forwarding setting and restore net.inet6.ip6.forwarding=0.
Remove the Recoilnet PF rules. Restore the backup only if no subsequent firewall
edits have been made. Validate with pfctl -nf before loading with pfctl -f.
